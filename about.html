A note from the founder

My name is Amam. I'm a father, a husband, and a son.

I started Consumer Rising for a pretty simple reason: I couldn't really read a food label. I'd turn a package over and find words I couldn't pronounce, numbers I didn't understand, and big promises on the front that had nothing to do with the ingredients on the back. And I thought — if I can't make sense of this, and I'm the one buying the groceries, something's off.

So I started reading. The actual studies. The regulator documents. The boring PDFs nobody opens. The more I read, the clearer it got: this stuff isn't written for regular people. It's written to be skimmed, not understood. Some of it would have to carry a warning label in Europe and sits on our shelves with nothing at all.

I'm not a doctor. I'm not a scientist. I'm just someone who got tired of not knowing what goes into his family's body and decided to do the homework in public.

That's what this is. I read the documents, I write down what I found in plain words, and I show you the sources so you can check me. When the science is solid, I'll say so. When it isn't, I'll say that too. No hype, no miracle claims, no pretending I know things I don't.

No sponsors, no advertisers, no food-industry money. Just the reading, and you.

If that sounds useful, stick around. It's free.
