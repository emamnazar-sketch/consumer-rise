A note from the founder

My name is Amam. I'm a father, a husband, and a son.

I started Consumer Rising for a pretty simple reason: the food system isn't written for regular people. Ingredient labels read like chemistry exams. Some additives that have to carry warning labels in Europe sit on American shelves with nothing at all. And the documents that explain it — the studies, the regulatory filings — are public, but nobody reads them for you.

So that's what this is. I read the documents, write down what I found in plain words, and show you the sources so you can check me. When the science is solid, I'll say so. When it isn't, I'll say that too. No hype, no miracle claims, no pretending to know things I don't.

I'm not a doctor or a scientist — I'm a reader doing the homework in public, one document at a time.

No sponsors, no advertisers. Just the reading, and you.

If that sounds useful, stick around. It's free.
